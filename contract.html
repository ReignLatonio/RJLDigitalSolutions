Terms and Conditions
1. Payment

a) 50% of the total fee is due upfront before work begins.

b) The remaining 50% is due upon delivery and client approval of the completed work.

c) For Full Suite subscriptions, the monthly fee of $500 USD is due on the same date each month. The subscription starts the day after this Agreement is signed.

d) All prices are quoted in USD. If paying in another currency, the amount will be converted at the current Wise exchange rate at the time of invoicing.

e) Payment can be made via PayPal or Wise. Payment details will be provided on the invoice.

f) If payment is not received within 7 days of the due date, the Provider reserves the right to pause all work until payment is received.

2. Revisions

Revision rounds included in this project: . Additional revisions beyond the included amount will be charged at $25 USD per hour. Revisions must be communicated in writing and consolidated into a single request per round where possible.

3. Timeline and Delivery

The Provider will make every reasonable effort to meet the estimated timeline. Delays caused by the Client (late feedback, missing content, unavailability) may extend the timeline. The Provider will communicate any delays promptly. For Full Suite clients, ongoing work is delivered on a rolling basis.

4. Additional Work

Any work outside the agreed scope will be discussed and quoted separately at $25 USD per hour.

5. Intellectual Property

Upon receipt of full payment, all deliverables become the property of the Client. The Provider retains the right to use the project in their portfolio and marketing unless the Client requests otherwise in writing. Third-party tools, platforms, and software (such as n8n, Google Workspace, etc.) remain subject to their own licensing terms.

6. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep confidential any proprietary information, business data, login credentials, and sensitive information shared during this engagement. This obligation survives termination and remains in effect indefinitely.

7. Communication

Primary communication will be via email and WhatsApp. The Provider will respond within 1 business day. Full Suite clients receive a dedicated monthly strategy call.

8. Cancellation, Refund, and Acceptance

a) Cancellation before work begins: full refund. By signing, the Client acknowledges the Provider may begin work immediately.

b) Cancellation after work begins: 50% upfront is non-refundable. Client must provide a valid written reason.

c) Client has 7 business days to approve or request revisions on each deliverable. No response within 7 days means automatic approval. Work matching the agreed scope is not refundable if rejected.

d) Provider cancellation requires a valid written reason. Client refunded for undelivered work. Third-party tools purchased by or for the Client are non-refundable by the Provider.

e) Full Suite subscriptions start the day after signing. Either party may cancel with 30 days written notice.

9. Limitation of Liability

The Provider's total liability shall not exceed the total amount paid by the Client. The Client is responsible for reviewing and approving all deliverables before publication or deployment.

10. Termination

Either party may terminate with 7 days written notice for material breach, with a 3-day cure period. During notice, the Provider is not obligated to deliver new work. Deliverables submitted but not responded to are considered accepted.

11. Entire Agreement

This constitutes the entire agreement. Amendments must be in writing and agreed by both parties.
