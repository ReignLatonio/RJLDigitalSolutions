1. Payment

a) 50% of the total fee is due upfront before work begins.

b) The remaining 50% is due upon delivery and client approval of the completed work.

c) For Full Suite subscriptions, the monthly fee of $500 USD is due on the same date each month. The subscription starts the day after this Agreement is signed.

d) All prices are quoted in USD. If paying in another currency, the amount will be converted at the current Wise exchange rate at the time of invoicing.

e) Payment can be made via PayPal or Wise. Payment details will be provided on the invoice.

f) If payment is not received within 7 days of the due date, the Provider reserves the right to pause all work until payment is received.

2. Revisions

Starter Package: 3 revision rounds. Growth Package: 5 revision rounds. Full Suite: Unlimited revisions.

A revision is a set of changes requested after a deliverable has been presented. Additional revision rounds will be charged at $25 USD per hour. Revisions must be communicated in writing and consolidated into a single request per round where possible.

3. Timeline and Delivery

The Provider will make every reasonable effort to meet the estimated timeline. Delays caused by the Client (late feedback, missing content, unavailability) may extend the timeline. The Provider will communicate any delays promptly.

4. Additional Work

Any work outside the agreed scope will be discussed and quoted separately at $25 USD per hour.

5. Intellectual Property

Upon receipt of full payment, all deliverables become the property of the Client. The Provider retains the right to use the project in their portfolio and marketing unless the Client requests otherwise in writing. Third-party tools, platforms, and software (such as n8n, Google Workspace, etc.) remain subject to their own licensing terms.

6. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep confidential any proprietary information, business data, login credentials, and sensitive information shared during this engagement. This obligation survives termination and remains in effect indefinitely.

7. Communication

Primary communication will be via email and WhatsApp. The Provider will respond within 1 business day. Full Suite clients receive a monthly strategy call.

8. Cancellation and Refund

a) If the Client cancels before work begins, a full refund of the upfront payment will be issued. By signing this Agreement, the Client acknowledges the Provider may begin work immediately.

b) Once work has commenced, the 50% upfront payment is non-refundable. The Client must provide a valid written reason for cancellation.

c) Upon delivery, the Client has 7 business days to approve or request revisions. No response within 7 days means automatic approval. If the Client rejects work that matches the agreed scope, the Provider is not obligated to issue a refund.

d) If the Provider cancels, a valid written reason must be provided. The Client will be refunded for undelivered work. Any third-party tools or subscriptions purchased by or on behalf of the Client are non-refundable by the Provider.

e) Full Suite subscriptions start the day after signing. Either party may cancel with 30 days written notice. No refunds for the current billing period.

9. Liability

The Provider's total liability shall not exceed the total amount paid by the Client. The Client is responsible for reviewing and approving all deliverables before they are published or deployed.

10. Termination

Either party may terminate with 7 days written notice if the other party breaches a material term and fails to remedy within 3 days. During the notice period, the Provider is not obligated to deliver new work. If the Client terminates after deliverables have been submitted but before responding, those deliverables are considered accepted.

11. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between both parties. Any amendments must be made in writing and agreed by both parties.
